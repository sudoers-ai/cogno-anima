# SUPEREGO — the voice

Moved verbatim from `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-11-08: the contract did not change. `CLAUDE.md` keeps a short summary and points here. Update this file in the same pull request as any change to the voice, as `CLAUDE.md` used to be updated.

- `voice(ctx, backend, *, voice_prompt) -> SuperegoResult` — **writes** `response` in the persona's voice **and limits** (limits go to the voice too, not only the judge), grounded in the tool data (exact figures verbatim); `strip_cot`; then a **deterministic envelope backstop** (`unwrap_envelope`, pure): a voicer sometimes answers `{"message": "..."}` and nothing between it and the contact opened it — measured live 2026-08-24 and RE-COUNTED 2026-08-26 — the denominator was the defect: only 9 of the box's 297 traces carry a `superego` block at all (persisted since 25/08 11:03), and **5 of those 9** show the flag, so "1 in 283" had divided by turns that predate the field; the rate among comparable turns is high on a small sample — and deterministic on the INPUT rather than random (the same sentence reproduced it 2/2, another gave plain text). It opens exactly ONE shape — an object whose single key is `message|text|reply|response|content` (the key VARIES between turns — measured `message` and `text` on the same process) and whose value is a string — and leaves every other shape alone, because picking a field out of a two-key object is guessing and a wrong guess ships the wrong text to a person (a BLANK value is left alone too — nothing downstream guards an empty response, measured across `cogno_soma.pipeline` and the host, so unwrapping it would trade visible garbage for unhandled silence); it flags `voice:json_unwrapped` so the trace can count how often the net is doing work (a net nobody counts becomes the mechanism). It runs BEFORE the other two backstops and that order is load-bearing: JSON escapes are legal, so `{"message": "CPF \\u0031\\u0032\\u0033.456.789-09"}` hides a CPF from the detector (measured `[]` raw, `['NATIONAL_ID']` unwrapped) — read the envelope and the leak ships unflagged. Then the **deterministic outgoing-PII backstop on the OUTPUT — REDACTION BY PROVENANCE** (2026-08-26, `security/redaction.py`, pure): the owner's rule is "PII may come IN, it must never go OUT", with the one exception that the CONTACT'S OWN datum may be said back to them — confirming the e-mail they just typed IS the answer. Every value the detector finds in the reply is decided against an allowlist and, when it acts, masked in place (`[EMAIL REDACTED]`); **it masks, it never refuses**, because a refusal costs the contact their answer and hands the turn to the re-voicing loop that has already been measured shipping a handoff instead of a booking. The allowlist has two halves: the CURRENT turn, derived here from `ctx.user_input`, and everything EARLIER in the session, injected by the host as `ctx.metadata[mk.PII_OUTPUT_ALLOWLIST]`. **Per SESSION, not per turn, and that is measured** (merged host bench #549, `a2aef70`, per SCENARIO — its earlier per-RUN headline was withdrawn by its own author as arithmetic): the per-turn variant breaks `own_email_recalled_three_turns_later` — the e-mail given at turn 1 and asked back at turn 3 — and no scenario is decided the other way. **The reader's role is the third input** (`mk.PII_READER_ROLE`, `Identity.role`; absent/blank/whitespace reads as GUEST): provenance alone is under-determined, and `tool_result_document_number` proves it — the tenant's own bookkeeper re-reading a ledger row he wrote has the SAME origin as a doctor's CPF handed to a patient, and the opposite correct answer. Measured deterministically over the merged answer key, the role bit moves agreement 5/7 → **6/7** and good-replies-broken 2/5 → **1/5**, leaks stopped 2/2 either way. **It ships in OBSERVATION mode** (`mk.PII_OUTPUT_MODE`, `vocab.VALID_PII_MODES`, default `observe`; a typo falls to observe, never to enforce): the one remaining break — the tenant's own reception number, `red_by_design` — is the same shape as the CNPJ and the CEP a scheduling persona says all day, against ZERO leaks in 297 production turns, so the rule decides everything, records `pii:would_redact_in_output` **plus the CLASS** (`pii:withheld_<type>`), and masks nothing. The class travels because the classes have OPPOSITE verdicts — a withheld `ADDRESS`/`TAX_ID` is almost always the tenant's own CEP/CNPJ (the frequent false positive), a withheld `NATIONAL_ID` is almost always a person who is not the one reading (the leak) — and counted together they decide nothing. The exit criterion is a CONSTANT, not a sentence in a PR: `vocab.PII_OBSERVATION_MIN_TURNS` = 200 — graduate a tenant when ≥200 turns **in which the detector FOUND PII in the reply** (`pii:flagged_in_output`) produce no `pii:withheld_*` of that tenant's own data (rule of three: zero in 200 bounds the rate under 1.5%). **The denominator is the turns with something to redact, never "SUPEREGO-carrying turns"** — the first wording counted those, and it is satisfied in the VOID: a reply with no personal data withholds nothing and can never be wrong, so 200 of them prove nothing came up, not that the rule redacts well (measured 2026-09-23: 0 replies with detected PII in 352 turns on the demo box — corpus: the box that day, 528 traces back to 2026-08-04). **Today NO tenant is ready** — none has a single turn with a finding. Graduation is PER TENANT and is a DECISION a human makes — nothing here promotes anybody. The decision is ONE pure function (`decide_provenance` over a `ProvenanceContext`) so the named-but-unbuilt second bit — tenant-DECLARED quotable contacts — enters as a field and a branch, not a rewrite. **The allowlist carries DIGESTS, never values**: `metadata` is read by the trace serialiser, by session-state persistence and by any guard that renders its kwargs into a log line, so a value-based allowlist would open a new store of cleartext PII to close one leak — and once inbound turns are stored masked (the agreed write-side follow-up, which must reuse THIS detector) values could no longer be rebuilt from history at all. Said plainly rather than implied: an unsalted SHA-256 over a phone number is **de-identification of the flow, not encryption** (same ceiling as the host's `scope_sha`) — a persisted digest is pseudonymous personal data and belongs inside the identity purge. Absence of the metakey makes the rule STRICTER, never off. The record is `SuperegoResult.pii_findings` (type + `vocab.VALID_PII_PROVENANCE` + verdict, **never the value**) plus the closed adjustment alphabet `pii:flagged_in_output` (kept, unchanged meaning) / `pii:redacted_in_output` or `pii:would_redact_in_output` (the MODE is in the TOKEN, not only in a config nobody reads back) / `pii:provenance_<value>` — every decision recorded, the allowed ones included, because "is the net getting in the way" needs the denominator and a redaction count alone has none. Graduation from `observe` to `enforce` is PER TENANT, decided by that production count — never a global flip. Synthesis drift and the preserved-term backstop both read the VOICED text, not the masked one: a mask is this guard's own doing, and feeding it back would make the protection look like a fabrication and could trip the correction loop against itself. `PiiDetector.find()` is the single definition — `detect()` is now expressed over it, so the flagging path and the masking path can never disagree) **and a preserved-term backstop** (flags `preserved:mutated_in_output` when a critical NOUMENO-preserved term — figure/email/URL — that the executor grounded shows up *altered* in the reply; flag-only, mutation-of-present not mere absence, so it never forces a nonsensical echo); feeds **lexical** `compute_synthesis` drift + recomputes cumulative. It also records `SuperegoResult.prompt_blocks` — the **inventory** of the voice prompt it ACTUALLY SENT (`voice_prompt_inventory`, pure): which top-level sections rendered, in order, and how long each was. Which blocks a turn got is the first question anyone asks about a bad reply and it was unanswerable after the fact, because the rendered prompt is deliberately not persisted (it is full of contact data — the same reason `mk.PROMPT_SHAS` digests the TEMPLATE and not the rendering). What makes the inventory safe to store is that its **output alphabet is closed**: the slugs come from `_VOICE_BLOCKS`, never from the text that matched, so a retrieved memory that forges a header can at worst shift a length — it can never contribute a byte of its own, and the block needs no purge path. It also records `SuperegoResult.prompt_text` — the rendered prompt itself, **in memory, never persisted by the core**: a defect that shows in one turn out of 283 cannot be diagnosed from block LENGTHS alone, and the host cannot rebuild what was assembled here and discarded. It is filled on EVERY call, not only an interesting one, because the turn that looks normal is the comparison half. A caller slices it with `voice_prompt_block(prompt, slug)`, which reads the SAME `_VOICE_BLOCKS` table — a host matching on the header text would be a second copy of a contract that has already changed once. The table is a DUPLICATED CONTRACT (it lives apart from `_build_voice_prompt`), so `tests/unit/test_voice_blocks_sync.py` pins both directions — every rendered header is listed, and every listed row is rendered — in the mould of `test_code_domains_match_prompt_domains_exactly`. Also surfaces a **register-accommodation** signal (`# Register`, derived from NER `parole`): match the user's formality *where it does not conflict with the persona* — **the persona voice/limits always win**. **Persona traits (2026-08-23):** the host stamps the persona's DECLARED traits in `ctx.metadata[mk.VOICE_TRAITS]` (closed `vocab.VALID_VOICE_TRAITS`, DERIVED from `VOICE_TRAIT_AXES` — `warm|reserved`, `formal|casual`, `concise|detailed`, `reserved|humorous` — plus the true singletons `direct` and `empathetic` — `humorous` sits on the `reserved` axis, and a test pins the disjunction). The PURE `vocab.sanitize_voice_traits(raw) → (kept, dropped)` (exported at the package root; the host's admin API refuses at save time exactly what it drops: unknowns, BOTH sides of a contradicting axis — resolved BEFORE the cap `MAX_VOICE_TRAITS`=4 —, a set is sorted first so every worker renders the same persona, a JSON-array string is decoded, garbage → `[]`, never raises) is applied by `persona_traits(ctx)`, which logs the drops once per configuration per process. `detect_adjustments` stays pure per-turn (no trait there — `general:review` keeps its meaning); `voice()` reads the traits once, MODULATES them in code (`_modulate_traits`, a pure table with two readings of the contact's emotion: **absolute = safety floor** — `humorous` leaves on a PII / de-escalation / FRUSTRATED-NEGATIVE / rejection-re-voice turn, `detailed` leaves on a re-voice, URGENT makes the reply `direct`+`concise`; **relative = personalization** — with `mk.CONTACT_STATE` (the host's per-identity EMA of `vocab.SENTIMENT_VALENCE`, ignored below `CONTACT_STATE_MIN_TURNS`=5) a NEGATIVE turn ≥ `CONTACT_ESCALATION_DELTA`=0.5 below the contact's own neutral offers `empathetic` (never to a `reserved` persona) and drops `detailed`, while the same FRUSTRATED turn from a contact whose neutral is already low keeps the persona's base tone — a chronic complainer is not in crisis at every message; a signal-less turn takes its tone from the neutral: warm ≥0.4 → `warm`, guarded ≤−0.4 → no humor. Two kinds of change to a DECLARED trait, stated once: **replaced** by the other side of its axis and only from the absolute branch (`add` — URGENT turns a `detailed` persona concise), or **dropped** when the turn forbids it (humour on a somber turn, detail on an escalation or a judge re-voice); NEVER dropped to fit a cap. The relative branch `offer`s instead — a courtesy that never overrides a declared opposite nor a declared `reserved` (`_EVEN_TRAIT`). A persona that declared NO traits gets none. **`_modulate_hints` is the other half, and the one that makes the relative reading visible:** `detect_adjustments` emits `tone:empathetic` for EVERY frustrated turn, so a contact whose normal IS upset was told "be empathetic" on every message — measured live 2026-08-24, the chronic complainer and the warm contact drew the SAME empathy, i.e. the traits table alone was a no-op. The contact's `register:*` line is suppressed only when it lies on the persona's declared formality axis, and the token then leaves the rendered hints too), passes them to `_build_voice_prompt` as a parameter, and appends `trait:*` to `SuperegoResult.adjustments` AFTER the prompt (audit trail; the rendered `Tone hints:` line stays the contact's). `_traits_section` renders a `# Voice for this turn (this persona's configured traits, adjusted for this message — obey)` section of its own — the header does not claim the tenant configured what the turn modulated — a standing instruction, not a background hint — framed as delivery-only (never figures/limits) and outranking the per-turn tone hints; a persona that declares `formal`/`casual` suppresses the contact's `User register:` line ONLY when that register is on the same (formality) axis — `technical`/`light`/`expressive` still reach the voice — and the token then leaves the rendered hints too. **`_baseline_signal`** states the comparison itself (the decision "this is/is not their normal" had no rendering at all, which is why the first two builds measured as no-ops), and obeys the SAME carve-outs the table does: no "warmly" for a persona declared `reserved`, and the escalation line asks for an acknowledgment *in* the reply, never before it (so it coexists with `direct`, exactly as the `empathetic` directive was worded to).
